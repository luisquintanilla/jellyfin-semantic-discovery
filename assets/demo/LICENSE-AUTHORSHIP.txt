Jellyfin Semantic Discovery PDF Corpus

Authorship
These three books contain original writing and original deterministic vector artwork created for
the Jellyfin semantic-discovery demonstration. Drafting and critique were AI-assisted; layout,
code validation, mechanical checks, and release preparation were deterministic local steps. The
technical source repositories are linked in each book and informed the subject matter; their text,
illustrations, and layouts were not copied wholesale.
